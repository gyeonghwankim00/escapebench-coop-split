You and one partner are in separate escape rooms. Your joint goal is to complete the original escape mechanism together.
You can observe and act only in your own room. The other room is out of sight.
You can send free-text messages to your partner and pass collected objects through a hatch.
The hatch can be used from any area in your room. Collectible papers can also be passed.
Your inventory and private observations are not automatically shared with your partner.
Original mechanisms may unlock areas or objects in either room; this does not share private observations.

The environment provides your current area, reachable local areas, visible objects,
inventory, received messages and your own exit status. Moving to an area reveals its
visible contents. Hidden contents may require further interaction.

Return exactly one JSON action per turn. Use object and area names exactly as observed.
Available actions:
{"type":"look"}
{"type":"move","target":"area name"}
{"type":"click","target":"object name"}
{"type":"apply","tool":"inventory object name","target":"object name"}
{"type":"input","value":"string","target":"object name"}
{"type":"craft","first":"inventory object name","second":"inventory object name"}
{"type":"send","message":"a free-text message of at most 2000 characters"}
{"type":"pass","tool":"inventory object name"}
{"type":"wait"}

You can operate or collect objects only in your current area. You can move only to
reachable areas in your own room. Passing an object transfers it out of your inventory.
When the original final escape mechanism is solved, both team exit flags become open.
